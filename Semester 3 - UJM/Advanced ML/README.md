Entire grade is based on reviewing a scientific paper from ICML of the current year.

You must choose a state-of-the-art paper from the ICML conference.

# Phase 1

Review this paper by writing 11-15 pages, the first 3-4 about summarising the paper, next 8-12 talk about 3 different topics that were covered during the course (RL, Optimal Transport, Physics-Informed ML, etc.).

Linking the paper to the topics is the hardest part, it's very hard to get more than 1 page linking it to a topic. I suggest to just throw a load of figures and formulas in there.

# Phase 2
After this, you submit your report. 2 of your classmates are then chosen to review your report. Submit your reviews.

# Phase 3
You will receive the 2 reviews, adjust your report accordingly, and then present to the class about your paper, how it is linked to course topics, and your critiques of the paper.

Pro tip is to read the reviews of the paper on OpenReview (I think it's called). I didn't do this, and Amaury asked everyone to give their personal opinion of the paper, so it would have been good to have read the reviews of some more experienced people.
