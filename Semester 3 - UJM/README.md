The semester was kind of broken into two parts for us.
- September, October and November were quite chill. We had one or two projects, and a few exams but nothing major.
- Everything was then packed into December and January. All the exams and all the project deadlines were in these months.

If it's a similar situation for you, I would honestly mention it to some of the teachers. Some of us told them already to change it for next year, so hopefully they learn.
