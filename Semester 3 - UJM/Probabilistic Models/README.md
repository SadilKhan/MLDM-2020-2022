Very difficult class, but just try to learn the core principles of each method he presents. He mainly asks about the overview of methods in the exam, instead of detailed math problems.

There is one lab session. He does surprise "exams" sometimes at the start of class, which may or may not count towards your final grade. They are just small probability problems.

There is also a project, ours was to implement Conditional Flow Matching and Empirical Flow Matching on the MNIST dataset. No presentation for this.

I think he changes the format of this class quite often, so may be different for every year.

**Note:** The exam from 24/25 is out of date, as he taught different material. He didn't teach us about Probabilistic Graphical Models this year, just Probabilistic Models.
